# Mishwar (مِشوار): lean plan

Approved 2026-10-05. Time box for all seven milestones: **2 hours**.

- **Stack:** Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Postgres with Prisma, Auth.js with Google
  sign-in, one Dokku app.
- **Product:** Arabic and right to left, works offline, built for iPhone first.
- **Rules live in `.claude/skills/`:** gaza-transit-domain, offline-first-pwa,
  ios-native-feel, ship-safely, taqat-deploy and on-air.

## Users

- **Guest:** no login. Sees vehicles on the map, searches and plans trips. Can't book.
- **Rider:**
  - books a seat and gets called by the driver;
  - shares their location only when the driver asks and they say yes;
  - posts special requests;
  - sees history and rates trips.
- **Driver:**
  - one vehicle (type, seats, photos, maintenance);
  - routes made of ordered stops, running both ways, with one default;
  - a weekly schedule (days, hours, route per day);
  - the driving screen that shares GPS;
  - confirms every rider before starting a trip;
  - sends offers on special requests.
- **One login screen for everyone:** «كمّل مع Google». First login picks rider or driver
  and asks for a phone number. The same Google account always comes back to the same role.

## Decisions

- **No route fares** anywhere. The only price is a driver's quote on a special-request
  offer.
- **Positions come from the driver's phone GPS only,** sent from the driving screen while
  it's open, with the screen kept awake. Never estimated from time or route.
  - Web apps can't send location in the background on iPhone or Android; native apps
    come later.
- **Only the latest position per vehicle is kept,** and its age is always shown. Older
  than 5 minutes is stale. Arrival time comes only from a fresh position.
- **Off-route alert:** if a vehicle with bookings is more than 500 m from its route for 2
  minutes, booked riders are told, with a call button.
- **Before starting a trip, the driver marks each rider ready or cancelled.** A cancel
  needs a call first, and the rider is told why.
- **Rider location** is shared only on a yes to each ask. It ends when the trip starts or
  after 15 minutes.
- **Photos are stored in Postgres,** because the server's container disk is wiped on every
  deploy.

## Data model

- **User:** role (set once), phone; plus Auth.js Account and Session.
- **Vehicle:** one per driver; type, seats, maintenance from/until.
- **Photo:** hash and the image itself.
- **Place:** OSM id, Arabic name, coordinates. Loaded from an OSM file committed to the
  repo.
- **Route and RouteStop:** several routes per driver, one default; ordered stops.
- **ScheduleDay:** weekday, working, hours, route.
- **VehiclePosition:** latest point, speed, time.
- **Booking:** status `pending`, `confirmed`, `ready`, `rejected` or `cancelled`; called,
  ready and cancel reason; started; off-route notified.
- **LocationShare:** asked, answer, expiry. The position is cleared at trip start or after
  15 minutes.
- **SpecialRequest, Offer (price), Message:** one chat thread per offer.
- **Rating:** 1–5 stars, both ways.
- **SearchLog:** anonymised; no user, no IP address.

## Screens

- **Everyone:** login; role and phone (first login only).
- **Guest and rider:**
  - map home (vehicles, data age, search);
  - trip planner;
  - booking with "where is it now" and arrival time;
  - location-consent sheet;
  - special request, offers, chat;
  - my trips with rating.
- **Driver:**
  - today (pre-trip check, start trip, ask for location);
  - driving screen «أنا سايق»;
  - vehicle;
  - routes;
  - week schedule;
  - open requests with offer and chat;
  - history with rating.

## What was really cut (as built, M1–M7)

The first cut list was a plan for running late. This is what happened.

**Cut:**

- **Route fares** (all milestones): by decision, not for time. The only price is a
  driver's quote on a special request.
- **MapLibre with a map file the app serves itself, and offline map download** (M3): the
  map is Leaflet over OpenStreetMap's own tiles, kept on the phone only as viewed. Areas
  outside the Strip are greyed out.
- **Picking stops by tapping the map** (M2): stops are picked from a searchable list of
  OpenStreetMap places; the map shows them afterwards.
- **A new route made on the spot inside the week schedule** (M2): make it in «خطوطي»
  first.
- **More than one change of vehicle** in the planner (M4).
- **The driver's one-tap "change today's route"** on an off-route alert (M5). Riders are
  still warned.
- **The nearby check on «ابدأ الرحلة»** (M5): it goes by time only.
- **The "N riders are affected" warning before saving** a schedule or maintenance change
  (M5): the bookings are cancelled on save and the driver is told how many afterwards.
- **Push notifications** (M5, M6, M7): people are told inside the app, at the next sync.
- **Older chat messages on demand** (M6): a thread shows its newest 30.
- **The rider's average shown to drivers, and a public list of comments** (M7): a comment
  is read only by the person it is about.
- **The iPhone install sheet, and dark mode beyond the colour tokens** (M1).

**Built although it was on the cut list:**

- The AI step in the planner (M4). It stays switched off until the server has an
  `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; it has not been tried against the real service.
- Live location updates while the rider's screen is open (M5).
- Chat on offers (M6).
- Extra routes and a different route per day (M2).
- Rating averages on driver cards (M7).
- The Wi-Fi-only photo queue (M2).

**Never checked on a real phone by the fleet:** Google sign-in end to end, the camera,
GPS while driving, the dialler coming back to the app, and installing to the home screen.
